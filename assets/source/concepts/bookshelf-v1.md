# Library bookshelf, visual revision 1

`bookshelf-multiview-v1.png` is an original four-view concept for one
institutional library bookshelf: a direct overhead view and three consistent
oblique views. It guides the long blue-grey steel frame, warm timber shelves
and irregular cream, rust, olive and navy books.

The runtime image is rendered from the 2 × 1 tile
`furniture.library.bookshelf` collection in
`assets/source/blender/environment.mvp.catalog.blend`. Two open rows of
individual book spines and three bays keep the function visible at 128 × 64
world pixels. The generated concept itself is never shipped as a sprite.
`object.bookshelf` retains its construction and education rules.
