# Institutional linoleum floor, visual revision 1

`institutional-floor-multiview-v1.png` is an original four-view material concept generated with OpenAI image generation on 2026-09-24: true overhead, low oblique detail, side section, and a 3 × 3 tiling preview. It informed the cool grey-green surface, subtle mineral flecks, fine traffic scuffs and square joints.

The playable `floor.linoleum.institutional` sprite is modeled and rendered in Blender from `environment.mvp.catalog.blend`; the concept PNG is reference only. It fills its 1 × 1 tile frame with no transparent margin. East and south joints meet adjacent copies. This visual replacement changes neither room zoning nor floor simulation.
