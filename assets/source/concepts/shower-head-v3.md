# Shower head refinement — 2026-09-24

`shower-head-multiview-v3.png` is an original generated reference of the same institutional shower fixture from overhead, front, side and oblique angles. The built-in image generator did not expose a backend model version. The generated sheet is for modelling only; its pixels are not shipped in the game.

The Blender model retains the buildable `object.shower-head` footprint of one tile and the existing `fixture.shower.head` art ID. A blue-grey enamel mounting plate, four dark bolts, brushed-metal supply arm, paired red and blue service marks, deep circular nozzle face, two rings of larger outlets and a teal rolled edge create the overhead hierarchy. A procedural noise material adds subtle wear to the enamel. The broad face and service colors were chosen to survive a 64×64 world-pixel view; no water effect or gameplay plumbing was added.

The committed runtime PNG comes only from the pinned Blender 5.2 orthographic pipeline. Object capabilities, room requirements and construction behavior stay unchanged.
