# Closed loading-dock gate, visual revision 1

`loading-dock-door-multiview-v1.png` is an original four-view reference: a
direct overhead study and three oblique views of a closed timber loading-dock
gate with steel tracks, visible plank seams and amber threshold reflectors.

The shipped sprite comes from the 3 × 1 tile
`furniture.delivery.dock_gate.closed` Blender collection. The concept is not
published as a runtime asset. `loading-dock-door-wooden` places
`object.loading-dock-door`, a tile-addressed delivery-bay requirement. The
simulation does not open this object or register it as a navigable door edge;
the fixed closed appearance deliberately makes no such promise.
