# Single cell bed, visual revision 2

`cell-bed-multiview-v2.png` is an original four-view concept for Lockstate.
Its overhead panel sets the silhouette; the oblique views guide the modelled
steel rails, corner posts and bedding depth. The grey mattress and muted orange
blanket swatches are `../textures/cell-bed-mattress-v1.png` and
`../textures/cell-bed-blanket-v1.png`, packed into the Blender scene.

Collection `furniture.cell.bed.single.variants` preserves its existing 1 × 2
tile footprint and north-facing pillow. The sprite is rendered from
`environment.mvp.catalog.blend` and published through the rendered-art catalog.
The earlier owner-supplied sheet remains in the source-art catalog for
provenance, but `env.object.bed` now reads the Blender render.
