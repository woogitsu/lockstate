# Cook role refinement

The original four-view reference is `cook-refinement-multiview-v1.png`.

The character keeps the existing rig, actor ID, jacket fabric, footprints, animation clips and runtime atlas contract. A warm ochre apron bib and wide straps separate the role from pale staff uniforms in game. The rounded cap has a broad teal stripe and ochre mark on its crown, visible from overhead. These large features are adapted in Blender; the reference is concept material only.
