# Classroom floor, visual revision 1

`classroom-floor-multiview-v1.png` is an original four-view concept generated
with Codex image generation on 2026-09-24. It is a reference for the Blender
model, never a runtime sprite. Pale oak-look resilient laminate gives the
education room a quiet, welcoming character beside its bookshelf and chairs.

The production `floor.classroom.oak-laminate` uses three narrow horizontal
planks per 1x1 game tile, staggered end joints and sparse fine grain. The
plank edges meet the next repeat. Their seams are restrained enough that a
5x5 Classroom should read as one floor at zoom 0.5 instead of a checkerboard.
The source color is warm enough to survive the existing blue room tint without
changing its gameplay meaning.

Concept prompt: A four-view architectural material reference showing the
same warm pale oak-look resilient laminate floor: top-down, low oblique,
macro grain and a repeated field at room scale. Long narrow planks, quiet
honey and greige wood grain, tiny staggered joins, soft satin finish. No
lettering, icons, logos, people, or square tile-sized seams.
