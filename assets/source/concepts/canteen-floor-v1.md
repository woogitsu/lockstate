# Canteen terrazzo floor v1

The playable `floor.canteen.terrazzo` frame is original Blender 5.2 geometry
in `environment.mvp.catalog.blend`, seen with the same orthographic overhead
camera as the environment catalog. A warm, washable stone-composite field has
fine recessed joints on its east and south edges and sparse mineral chips.
The field reaches every edge of the 1 × 1 tile so adjacent frames repeat
without gaps. The chips are restrained at the game's 64 px tile size to leave
food-service furniture and the Canteen room label readable.

This floor belongs only to `room.canteen`. The Kitchen remains on its
blue-grey non-slip tile, and other zoned rooms remain on institutional
linoleum. Both food rooms keep their established zoning tints and room-name
labels. The intended distinction is material, not a stronger tint wash.
