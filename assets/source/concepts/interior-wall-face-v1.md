# Interior wall face, visual revision 1

`interior-wall-face-multiview-v1.png` is an original four-view concept generated with OpenAI image generation on 2026-09-24. It shows one repeatable front elevation, the overhead coping, an oblique section and three front modules tiled together. The concept guides the pale plaster, cool enamel coping, restrained panel joint and dark skirting.

The playable `wall.interior.face` sprite is authored as a shallow Blender relief in `environment.mvp.catalog.blend`, viewed orthographically from above to fit the game's 2D elevation projection. The concept PNG is reference only. Geometry fills a 1 × 1 tile frame with zero transparent margin; continuous horizontal bands meet at tile boundaries. This replaces the prior cropped sheet for east-west interior walls without changing construction rules.
