# Cell floor, visual revision 1

`cell-floor-multiview-v1.png` is an original image-generation concept from
2026-09-25. It is reference material for Blender, not a runtime sprite.

The concept compares a true overhead surface, a low oblique section, close
aggregate and a repeated field. Its repeated-field panel shows square joints;
those are deliberately omitted from the Blender model. At the game's 64-pixel
tile size they would form a distracting grid beneath beds, toilets and room
labels. The production tile uses pale cool grey sealed concrete, subtle
mineral variation and fine embedded aggregate, and keeps the surface opaque
and continuous at every edge.

Production ID: `floor.cell.sealed-concrete`, one by one game tile. Standard
and solitary cells use it; the holding cell retains the shared institutional
floor. The existing zoning tints and all room labels stay unchanged.

Concept prompt: Original four-panel material study of a pale cool grey sealed
concrete prison cell floor for Blender modeling: true overhead seamless square
surface, low oblique material section, close embedded aggregate, and a three
by three repeat. Restrained institutional style, even overhead light, tiny
mineral flecks and faint wear. No people, furniture, room labels, logos or
copied game assets. No visible module-edge seams in the production model.
