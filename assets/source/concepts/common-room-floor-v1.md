# Common Room floor, visual revision 1

`common-room-floor-multiview-v1.png` is an original four-view concept generated
with Codex image generation on 2026-09-24. It guides the Blender model, not
the runtime sprite. A warm resilient cork-rubber composite supports the two
wooden benches and makes recreation feel more welcoming than the institutional
linoleum. Fine recycled ochre and charcoal granules keep the material visible
at game scale without competing with room names.

The production `floor.common-room.cork-rubber` is a fully opaque, seamless
1x1 tile. Its base shader is periodic at opposite edges and the inset granules
never touch the rim. There are no symbols or tile-sized seams, so a 5x5 room
does not become a distracting checkerboard at zoom 0.5.

Concept prompt: Original four-view architectural material sheet of a warm
honey-grey resilient cork-rubber composite: orthographic top, low oblique,
macro granules, and a 3x3 repeat. Fine warm ochre and charcoal recycled
granules, soft satin finish, restrained modern institutional style. No
furniture, people, walls, lettering, logos, icons, or tile-sized seams.
