# Employee office desk, visual revision 2

`employee-desk-multiview-v2.png` is an original four-view reference generated
for Lockstate. `../textures/employee-desk-laminate-v1.png` is its companion
grey-oak laminate swatch. The concept determines the worktop, edge band,
steel frame, distinct three-drawer pedestal and restrained paperwork details.

Blender collection `furniture.office.desk.employee.variants` keeps its existing
2 × 1 tile footprint and asset ID. Its drawer pedestal projects past the
worktop enough to stay separate in the overhead game view. The swatch is packed
into `environment.mvp.catalog.blend`; the game uses the rendered sprite.
