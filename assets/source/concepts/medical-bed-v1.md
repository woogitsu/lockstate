# Medical bed, visual revision 1

`medical-bed-multiview-v1.png` is an original four-view reference for
Lockstate's infirmary. The head-up medical silhouette, white segmented safety
rails and marked foot panel distinguish it from the ordinary cell bed when
viewed from above. `../textures/medical-bed-teal-fabric-v1.png` supplies the
washable teal cover and is packed into the Blender scene.

`furniture.medical.bed.single` is a separate 1 × 2 tile collection in
`environment.mvp.catalog.blend`. The game uses its rendered sprite for
`object.medical-bed`; the buildable, its footprint and medical-treatment
capability remain unchanged.
