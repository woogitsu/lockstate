# Dirt–grass boundary overlays for the rotating world

The real `ObliqueWorldScene` browser harness, fed an 8×8 dirt/grass boundary without changing repository scene files, shows a perfectly straight color cut at yaw −90°, −45°, 0°, 45°, and 90°. A corridor floor module already exists, so this is the larger missing art treatment. The live app has no terrain paint UI; the browser-only fixture replacement in `tooling/qa-live-oblique-grass-seam.mjs` is the reproducible scene evidence.

The Blender source adds four transparent ground-level overlays. Each lies across one side of a **grass tile facing dirt**: a restrained ragged soil/thatch band and small turf tips cross the former straight edge. The side ends join when repeated along a run. A grass tile at a corner can receive two overlays; the 11×11 patch below exercises all four directions and its corners.

The source is `assets/source/blender/floor.terrain.dirt-grass.edge.blend`, generated by `tooling/blender/build-dirt-grass-edge.py`. Each manifest has 24 yaw × 3 elevation transparent frames, a 512 px image, a ground pivot at `(256,256)`, and 64 nominal pixels per tile. The game scene must paint the base `floor.terrain.grass` first, then the chosen overlays above terrain sprites. These catalogs alone do not change the live app.

## Full HD visual gate

The live views use the real scene with its present hard boundary. The before/after views use a browser composition of the Blender dirt and grass base tiles at 1920×1080, elevation 45°, 64 px/tile; only the directional overlays change. At yaw 0° and 90° the line breaks into a thin irregular border without a raised lip. At diagonal yaw the four edges remain aligned; the inside of the grass patch stays visually quiet.
