# What stage 6 (#1162) would actually change (2026-09-14)

Issue [#1162](https://github.com/woogitsu/lockstate/issues/1162), stage 6 of
the identity rollout (`docs/IDENTITY_V5_ROLLOUT.md` §"Stage 6 — Language"). It
asks two things: does the 466-string English catalogue already match the
delivery's voice direction, and which of its sentences are claims the code may
not be able to prove — `AGENTS.md`'s fourth reservation makes the *truth* of a
player-visible sentence the owner's, and constitution article 5 (*"Każde zdanie
jest prawdziwe"*) is the delivery's version of the same rule. This pass changes
no string; it measures. Checked against the tree at `236bed4e`, worktree
`/workspace/saves-panel`.

## 1. What the delivery actually requires, quoted

The direction is Polish; the shipped game is English. Three sources carry the
requirement, and they agree with each other:

**The voice pattern**, `DOKUMENTACJA/projekt.md:99-116`:

> Wzór: **fakt → miejsce → następny krok**.
>
> Używamy bezpośrednich czasowników: Wybierz, Sprawdź, Zaplanuj, Zapisz. Nie
> karcimy gracza. Nie żartujemy z cierpienia. Nie przedstawiamy braku danych
> jako dobrego stanu.

("Pattern: fact → place → next step. We use direct verbs: Choose, Check, Plan,
Save. We do not scold the player. We do not joke about suffering. We do not
present an absence of data as a good state.")

Its own ten-row example table (`projekt.md:105-115`) is what issue #1162 calls
"a set of shapes; its numbers are invented and the delivery says so" — every
row pairs a **situation**, a **message**, and an **action verb button**:

| Situation | Message | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brak dostępu (No access) | Nie ma przejścia do celi A-12. Sprawdź drzwi i połączenie z korytarzem. | Pokaż przejście (Show the passage) |
| Zapis potwierdzony (Save confirmed) | Zapisano na tym urządzeniu o 14:32. | Pokaż zapisy (Show saves) |
| Pusta lista (Empty list) | Nie masz jeszcze planów budowy. | Zaplanuj pierwszy element (Plan the first item) |

The last line of that section: *"Liczby są przykładowe. Komunikaty i akcje
wymagają dowodu w kodzie przed integracją."* ("The numbers are examples. The
messages and actions need proof in the code before integration.") — the
delivery states the reservation itself.

**Constitution article 5**, `DOKUMENTACJA/konstytucja.md:27`:

> Nie mów „zapisano", zanim zapis zostanie potwierdzony. Nie mów „wolne
> miejsce", jeśli znana jest wyłącznie liczba łóżek. „Brak incydentów" i „brak
> danych" to różne stany. Nigdy nie obiecuj synchronizacji w chmurze bez
> wdrożonego przepływu.

("Do not say 'saved' before the save is confirmed. Do not say 'free space' if
only the bed count is known. 'No incidents' and 'no data' are different
states. Never promise cloud sync without an implemented flow.")

**Constitution article 9**, `konstytucja.md:47`, the register rather than the
shape:

> Używamy języka rzeczowego i szanującego osadzonych oraz personel. Nie
> żartujemy z cierpienia i nie infantylizujemy zagrożeń. Rozgrywka może mieć
> trudne konsekwencje, ale komunikat nie karci gracza i nie robi z ludzi
> zasobów w warstwie narracyjnej.

("We use factual, respectful language about prisoners and staff. We do not
joke about suffering and do not infantilize threats. Play may have hard
consequences, but the message does not scold the player or turn people into
resources at the narrative layer.")

Issue #1162 itself restates article 5's three anti-patterns and adds the
mechanics already in force: stable keys, whole sentences, no concatenation,
Polish plural forms, locale-formatted numbers/dates, and a determinism rule
(simulation code may branch on a stable id and must never read, persist, hash
or compare translated text).

## 2. What `default-locale-en.ts` says today, measured

**Count: exactly 466 authored sentences**, not sampled — `docs/PLAYER_STRINGS.md`
is generated byte-for-byte from `src/content/default-locale-en.ts` by
`tooling/player-string-inventory.mjs` and pinned by
`tests/foundation/player-string-inventory-contract.test.ts`, so its own count
(`docs/PLAYER_STRINGS.md:25`, "The 466 authored sentences") is exact rather than
estimated, and this pass re-derived the same number independently:
`grep -c "^  '" src/content/default-locale-en.ts` also returns 466.

**Categories, by key prefix** (grep over the generated key list, not hand
classification — counts do not sum to 466 because some prefixes were grouped
and roughly 49 single-off keys were left unbucketed rather than force-fit):

| Bucket | Count | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `hud.rooms.*` + `hud.build.*` + `hud.security.*` + `hud.regime.*` + `hud.intake.*` | 155 | panel labels, hints, catalogue rows |
| `hud.alert.refusal.*` (any depth) | 64 | "The build order failed — that order already exists." |
| `save.*` | 65 | save/load/delete/import status lines |
| `hud.alert.event.*` | 27 | "Payday went unpaid — your staff are owed {total}." |
| `room.*.name` / `object.*.name` | 45 | "Cell", "Bed" — catalogue nouns, not sentences |
| `hud.status.*` / `hud.clock.*` / `hud.tab.*` / `hud.zoom.*` / `hud.minimap.*` / `hud.transport.*` | 38 | chrome labels |
| `brand.*` / `display.*` | 12 | theme and brand strings |
| `input.action.*` | 11 | control labels ("Decrease {field}") |

**Sampling method, stated honestly.** This is not a complete audit of every
string against the voice pattern. It is a stratified read: every key in the
two largest sentence-bearing buckets — `hud.alert.refusal.*` (64) and
`hud.alert.event.*` (27) — was read in full (both are short enough to `grep`
whole), plus a targeted read of `save.status.*` (27, all read), `hud.regime.*`
(all read), and a handful of `hud.rooms.*` rows chosen because an earlier
research record (`2026-09-06-the-first-furnished-cell.md`) had flagged a
capacity-reporting risk in that area. `hud.build.*` (50 keys) and
`hud.security.*` (30 keys) beyond the rows already quoted below were not read
line by line; a reader who wants the full picture there should grep
`docs/PLAYER_STRINGS.md` directly rather than trust this pass exhausted it.

**What already matches the direction, on the sampled set.** The
fact-then-consequence shape is nearly universal in `hud.alert.refusal.*` and
`hud.alert.event.*` — every refusal row reads `<outcome> — <reason>` (e.g.
`hud.alert.refusal.build.water-blocked`: `'The build order failed — there is
water on that tile.'`, `default-locale-en.ts:656`) and every event row reads
`<what happened> — <consequence>` (e.g. `hud.alert.event.economy.wages-unpaid`:
`'Payday went unpaid — your staff are owed {total}.'`, `:1085`). Article 9's
register (factual, not scolding, no joking about suffering) held on every
string read. `save.status.saved`, `.quota-exceeded`, `.transaction-aborted`
and `.changed-elsewhere` (`:3164-3185`) carry inline comments citing the exact
code that makes each one true, including one documented case
(`save.status.changed-elsewhere`, ADR 0109 Decision 5) where a warmer
candidate sentence — *"Your progress here is safe; try again"* — was written,
considered, and **rejected** specifically because "safe" was a promise about
the player's memory that "nothing on the save path verifies" (comment at
`:3174-3182`). That is article 5 already being enforced by hand, ahead of the
delivery that named it a constitution article.

**What does not match the shape.** None of the 64 refusal rows or 27 event
rows sampled carry the delivery's third element — an attached **next-step
action verb** wired to a control, the way every row of `projekt.md`'s table
pairs a message with an action ("Pokaż przejście", "Zaplanuj pierwszy
element"). The English catalogue states fact and consequence; it does not
name a next step, and no code was found that attaches a "jump to this
location" or "open this panel" control to an alert or refusal row. This is
the largest single gap against the direction, and it is a UI-wiring gap, not
a wording gap — see §4.

## 3. Sentences the code may not be able to prove

The most valuable list this document can carry, per the task brief and per
`AGENTS.md`'s fourth reservation. Two tiers: one demonstrated by reading the
producing code, one that is a structural risk stage 6 would create if it
added the delivery's action-verb pattern without also wiring the actions.

### 3a. A live risk in the current catalogue, cited

**`'hud.alerts.empty'`: `'No active alerts'`** (`src/content/default-locale-en.ts:568`).

This is exactly the pair article 5 and the delivery's voice section both name
by example — *"'Brak incydentów' i 'brak danych' to różne stany"* / *"Nie
przedstawiamy braku danych jako dobrego stanu"* — and the code does not keep
them apart for this string.

- `HudViewModel.alerts` has one producer path traced end to end: `src/main.ts:1199`
  initializes `let viewModel: HudViewModel = EMPTY_HUD_VIEW_MODEL` before any
  worker message has arrived, and `EMPTY_HUD_VIEW_MODEL.alerts` is the literal
  `[]` (`src/ui/hud/view-model.ts:2130`). Its own doc comment
  (`view-model.ts:2064-2071`) states the intent for the *clock* field
  explicitly: *"An empty prison, for a first paint before any snapshot has
  arrived. The clock reads unknown, not 'day 1, paused, at the start of the
  day'... a confident readout of a clock that is not running is the exact
  failure the transport controls used to have: something on screen that looks
  like state and is not."* — and gives the clock its own `UNKNOWN_HUD_CLOCK`
  sentinel for exactly that reason.
- **`alerts` gets no equivalent sentinel.** It stays the literal `[]`, which is
  indistinguishable from "the worker reported zero alerts" — the render path
  (`src/ui/hud/hud.ts:2509-2517`) tests only `viewModel.alerts.length === 0`
  and paints `hud.alerts.empty` whenever that is true, with no branch for "no
  snapshot has arrived yet."
- `src/ui/simulation-alerts.ts:205-211`'s own docblock confirms the field had
  **no producer at all** until #261: *"`HudViewModel.alerts` had no producer
  before this... the only assignment to the field anywhere in `src/` was the
  literal `[]` in `EMPTY_HUD_VIEW_MODEL`."* — i.e. the authors already knew
  this field's pre-worker value was a placeholder, for the same reason the
  clock's was, and gave the clock a fix but not this field.

**What this is and is not.** It is a measurement, not a diagnosis, per
`docs/AGENT_WORKFLOW.md` §3: this pass did not drive a browser to time the
window between first paint and the first worker snapshot, so whether a player
can actually see `'No active alerts'` flash before real data arrives — and for
how long — is unmeasured. What is established by reading the code is that the
render path has no way to distinguish the two states for this one field, while
it deliberately does for the sibling field the same empty view model carries.
That asymmetry, on its own, is the finding.

### 3b. What stage 6 would create if it added the pattern literally

The delivery's own three-column table pairs every message with a **pressable
action** ("Pokaż przejście", "Zaplanuj pierwszy element", "Sprawdź wersje").
Constitution article 6 is explicit that this is not optional decoration:

> Przycisk naprawczy musi mieć rzeczywistą implementację; inaczej tekst tylko
> wyjaśnia stan.

("A repair button must have a real implementation; otherwise the text only
explains the state.") Searched for a producer of that action column and found
none: no code under `src/ui/` attaches a "jump to tile/room" or "open this
panel" handler to a refusal or alert row. `src/ui/simulation-alerts.ts` (§2
above) builds text rows only; `hud.ts:2509-2517`'s row renderer takes a label
and an icon, no destination. So **the third column of the delivery's table is
the one piece stage 6 cannot author as a string at all** — it needs a
navigation mechanism (locate the tile/room a refusal or event names and focus
the camera or open the relevant panel on press) that does not exist yet, and
authoring an action-verb label ("Show the passage") on a button that does
nothing on press would be exactly the anti-pattern article 6 names, worse than
today's status quo of no button at all.

### 3c. Checked and found clean

Listed because a document that only reports suspicions is not trustworthy
about the things it did check. No `account.*` or `cloud.*` locale key exists
(`grep -n "'account\.\|'cloud\." src/content/default-locale-en.ts` returns
nothing), so article 5's fourth named anti-pattern — promising cloud sync
without an implemented flow — has nothing to check yet; this matches
`docs/research/2026-09-13-what-the-saves-panel-may-say.md`'s §3 finding,
re-confirmed today (`grep -rn "createClient\|VITE_SUPABASE" src/` still
empty). `hud.rooms.at-capacity*` (the capacity-reporting area an earlier
record flagged risk in) is scoped to **finished** rooms only —
`roomNeedsSubjectOf`'s doc comment (`src/ui/hud/rooms-panel.ts:431-432`):
*"every room is finished and at least one of them cannot take another user
right now"* — so it does not repeat the ghost-bed-counts-as-capacity risk that
record found in a different counter (`roomCapacity`, which this pass did not
re-open). `hud.security.coverage-unguarded-consequence` ("No guard is posted
here, so nobody in this sector is kept safe.") carries a 30-line inline proof
(`default-locale-en.ts:2617-2649`) tracing the exact simulation constant
(`SAFETY_COVERAGE_PROVISION_MULTIPLIER.unguarded === 0`) that makes it true and
naming what it deliberately does not claim (it is not a claim about incidents,
per a prior owner wording that was refused for exactly that overreach, #848 /
PR #854).

## 4. What stage 6 would cost, in separable pieces

1. **Author `docs/LOCALIZATION.md` voice-rule additions** — the fact/place/
   next-step pattern, the four named anti-patterns, and the register from
   article 9 — as prose guidance for authors, since `docs/PLAYER_STRINGS.md`
   cannot carry it (generated, byte-pinned to the tooling). No `src/` change;
   pure documentation, reviewable on its own.
2. **Give `HudViewModel.alerts` its own pre-snapshot sentinel**, mirroring
   `UNKNOWN_HUD_CLOCK`, and a render branch in `hud.ts` for it — a
   `hud.alerts.unknown` (or similarly named) string distinct from
   `hud.alerts.empty`, closing §3a. Small, isolated to
   `src/ui/hud/view-model.ts`, `src/ui/simulation-alerts.ts` and
   `src/ui/hud/hud.ts`; needs its own red-then-green test per
   `docs/AGENT_WORKFLOW.md` §3 before it can be reported fixed. **Two other
   agents are currently working in `src/ui/hud/` and `src/input/`** per this
   session's brief — this piece is out of bounds for whoever picks it up
   until that work lands, and should be sequenced after.
3. **Design and build the "next step" navigation mechanism** §3b identifies as
   missing — locate the tile/room a refusal or event names, focus the camera
   or open the relevant panel on a press. This is the expensive piece: it is
   new UI plumbing, not a string change, and it is a precondition for
   authoring any action-verb label from the delivery's table. Likely needs its
   own design pass (which refusals/events get an action, what each one
   focuses) before implementation — a candidate for an ADR if the mapping
   between refusal/event kind and navigation target turns out non-trivial.
4. **Author the `pl` locale catalogue** — issue #1162's own "known outstanding
   and larger than it looks" item: there is no `pl` catalogue in the tree at
   all (confirmed: no `src/content/*locale-pl*` or similar file exists), only
   the plural mechanism. Already tracked as #661. Independent of 1-3 and by
   far the largest of the four pieces by string count (466 sentences to
   translate and have reviewed for the same truth constraint, in the language
   the constitution itself is written in).
5. **A register pass over the existing 466**, checking article 9's tone
   requirement string by string rather than the sampled subset this pass
   read. Low expected yield given what was sampled (§2), but "low expected
   yield" is not "checked" — this pass read roughly 190 of 466 keys in full
   (the refusal, event, save-status and regime buckets) and left `hud.build.*`
   and `hud.security.*` beyond the one cited row unread.

None of 1-5 requires a decision from the owner under `AGENTS.md`'s standing
mandate — each is an ordinary increment once written, and each string any of
them adds still needs its code citation quoted in the commit and PR body per
the fourth reservation.

## Weakest claim in this pass

§3a's finding is read off the code, not observed in a running browser: the
asymmetry between `UNKNOWN_HUD_CLOCK` and the bare `[]` for `alerts` is real
and the two doc comments quoted are real, but whether a player's eye can
actually register `'No active alerts'` during the gap — as opposed to it being
theoretically reachable but too brief to matter, the way `docs/AGENT_WORKFLOW.md`
§3's "a duration recorded against a test in a cancelled job" entry warns
against over-reading a number nobody watched — was not measured here. What
would change this assessment: a Playwright run instrumented to capture the
HUD's alerts-list DOM in the first frame after mount and before the first
`protocol` worker message, on a throttled connection where the gap is wide
enough to observe. §2's category counts are a second, smaller weak point: the
eight-bucket table does not sum to 466 by construction (stated in the table's
own note) and a reader wanting an exact per-prefix breakdown should regrep
rather than trust the rounding here.
