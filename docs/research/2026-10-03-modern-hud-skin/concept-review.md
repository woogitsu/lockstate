## What the image actually suggests

Its interface has a calm neutral frame, strong navy text, selective teal selection and comparatively little nested box decoration. Much of its appeal comes from tactile furniture, softened architectural edges and warm scene lighting. Those are real model/material/rendering work, not effects this CSS skin can prove or supply. The bitmap also shows only two top readings and illustrative controls. Keep all nine real readings and the approved status palette; do not copy its omissions, simplified layout, invented scene content or apparent geometry as product evidence.
